# Office announcement — PRSFlo launch (draft for Eli to edit + send)

Subject: PRSFlo goes live [DATE]

Team —

PRSFlo — our studio operations app — goes fully live on [DATE]. This is the
system we run the studios on from here forward: sessions, work orders,
checklists, tasks, flags, billing. One app, everyone's day in it.

**Runners:** you each have your OWN login now — I'll hand you your email +
password personally. Sign in at prsflow.paramountrecording.com on your phone
and add it to your home screen. Your studio page has an **App Guide** at the
bottom — two minutes, read it on your first shift. The one rule worth knowing
on day one: submit your day when it's done, and if something changes after,
just fix it and hit Update submission. You can always fix your day until the
office approves it.

**Office:** the dashboard is your morning read — Flo's briefing tells you
what needs you. Billing lives in the Billing hub; the manager's morning is
the Daily Ops page. Operating manuals for both roles are being finalized this
week and will live in the app.

**Everyone:** you'll see small blue **?** circles around the app — those are
helpful hints. Tap one for a tip. They're on for everyone to start; turn them
off any time in Settings (bottom of the left menu) or the 💡 on the runner
page.

Two asks for week one: record things when they happen (times, payments,
problems), and if something in the app looks wrong or confusing, say so —
there's a DEV feedback button in Settings, and I read every note.

— Eli
