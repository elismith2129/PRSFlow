Approval row: the amount, and where the money stands

Three rows waiting. Two are paid in full, one has $190 owed. Today every row shows only its total, so a paid COD and an unpaid one read the same. The AR convention is Amount · Paid · Balance Due, with Balance Due carrying the weight. Three ways to put that on a one-line row.

A · Total, with the balance under it

Recommended

The total stays as the headline, because that is the figure you are approving. A second small line says where the money stands: Paid in full in green, $190.00 due in red, and for a label invoice that hasn't gone out yet, nothing (the balance is the total; saying so is noise). The total dims once it's settled, the same cue the list rows below the strip already use. Built already.

The pick
  • A. It answers both questions on one line — what am I approving (the total) and is money owed (the subline) — and it matches how the list rows already read, so the strip and the list stop disagreeing.
  • Red on the subline means "collection was missed", the same meaning red has everywhere else on COD. Green "Paid in full" means the only thing left is your signature.
  • The same two lines go on the small cards across the top of the package window, so you see it there too.
The rules behind the subline
  • Balance ≤ 0 and total > 0 → Paid in full (green). Total dims.
  • Part paid → $paid paid · $due due (red).
  • COD, nothing collected → $total due (red).
  • Billing, not yet sent → no subline. The balance is the total; the row is about approval, not collection.
  • A total that changed since the invoice went on keeps its red ⚠ as today — that outranks everything.
