Runner work order — list ⇄ cards

One design, three states. Same component as the admin work order — runner mode locks the office card, the rates and any approved day; everything else stays live. Phone 1 is the list view (the §16 day blocks — compact, scans well when a session runs many days). Phone 2 is card view — one day, one card, no sideways scrolling. Phone 3 is what tapping a card opens: a bottom sheet with big inputs for the five things a runner actually types. The toggle above the table switches views, Finder-style; tap the toggle and the cards in phones 1–2 — they work. Payments stay editable (COD at the desk); Submit is the only terminal act.
