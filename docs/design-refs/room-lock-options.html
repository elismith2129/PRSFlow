Room lock — lock or unlock a room's Mac from PRSFlo

This round: manual Lock and Unlock per room, screen only, tested on one room. Owners and managers only. Every lock and unlock is logged with who and when.

Part 1 · Option A — one card per room

Recommended

Why: one big button per room, hard to hit the wrong one at 3am on a phone. A locked room has a red outline you can see from across the office. Every card is the same fixed size. The dot shows whether that room's Mac is listening; a room with no Mac set up yet has its button greyed out.

Covers every screen, sits above Pro Tools, blocks keyboard and mouse, mutes the Mac. It clears when the room is unlocked in PRSFlo, or when staff types their PIN on the room's keyboard. The PIN is the backup for when the internet is down.
