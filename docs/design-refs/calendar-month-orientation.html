Month Orientation — 3 Options

The infinite strip has no "page," so the eye needs an anchor. Scroll each demo sideways (Jul → Aug → Sep) and feel the difference. They can also combine.

A — Sticky month rail

A thin band above the days: each month is a segment as wide as its days, and its name STAYS PINNED at the left edge while you're inside it — then the next month's name pushes it out, exactly like section headers in a phone contact list. You always know where you are without reading a date. (Alternating months get a whisper of tint here too.)

B — Live month chip

No new band: a single floating Archivo chip pinned top-left of the calendar that live-updates to whatever month is under the viewport as you scroll. Zero vertical cost; the answer is always in one fixed place. (Chip could live in the toolbar instead.)

JUL

C — Tinted months, passive

No band, no chip: alternate months sit on a slightly different ground tone, the boundary line is heavier, and each month's "1" carries its initials ("AUG 1"). Purely passive — you sense the month change like a paper stock change. Subtlest; weakest for the "scroll fast to October" case on its own, best as a companion to A or B.

Recommendation: A is the strongest single answer (the name is glued to the viewport while its days pass). A + C's tint together is the full fix. B is the cheapest to build if vertical space is precious.
