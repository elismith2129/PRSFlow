Unsubmitted work orders — caught the night of

The reminder lives in the two moments the runner is still in the building: when the session ends and when they close the studio. Nothing new to record — a day's rows are born in progress and Submit flips them; the app just starts reading that the same night instead of never.

1When the session ends Night of

Once the booked end time passes (or the runner types a "Left" time), the hub card's grey "Not submitted" turns red and says so, and the red strip appears at the top of the hub — the same night, while they are still there. It clears the moment they submit.

3The office side (comes with it)

Your dashboard statement gets an anomaly bullet — always shows, red, names the studio, the day, the client, and who closed that studio that day (from their daily-ops submission). Billing → Awaiting gets a "Not submitted" pill on those WOs so nobody chases money on a day nobody looked at.
