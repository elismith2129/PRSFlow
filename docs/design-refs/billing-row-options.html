Billing list — status pills, sortable headers, day dividers

Three changes to the billing side of the hub. The pill is derived from what the row already knows (its step, plus a standing rejection) — no new state, no migration. Headers sort only. Day dividers group by session date.

A · The pill names WHERE THE ROW IS STUCKRecommended
Inside one tab every row shares the bucket, so a bucket badge would print “In progress” ten times. What you actually scan for is which of those ten are waiting on you: Needs invoice (billing’s), Needs approval (the owner’s), Ready to send, Returned (bounced, billing’s again). Same colours as everywhere else — amber waits on a person, teal is clear to go, hot is wrong. Day dividers by session date; headers sort on click (arrow shows the active column, click again to reverse).

The pill replaces nothing — the three lights stay. Lights show how far along the line a row is; the pill names the ONE thing it is waiting on, which is what the eye is hunting when you scan a tab. Sent rows carry the blue “Sent · aging” pill in Awaiting payment; Paid and Closed rows wear no pill (there is nothing left to be stuck on).
B · The pill names the BUCKET (the literal COD parallel)
Exactly what COD does — the row wears its bin’s colour and name. On COD that earns its place because several bins are latched into one merged list. On billing you are always looking at one bucket, so it prints the tab’s own name on every row. Shown to record why A wins; the one place this shape is right is SEARCH, where results span every bucket — and the hub already does that today.

Sorting — and what it does to the day dividers
Click a header to sort, click again to reverse; the active column keeps the arrow. WO · Client · Status · Balance · Age are sortable — “Next” is not, because it is derived from the step and sorting by it would just be sorting by Status with a confusing name.

Day dividers appear only while the list is sorted by date — the default. Sort by Balance or Age and they switch off, because a “Tue, Sep 2” heading over rows ordered by money is a heading that lies about what follows it. Sort back to Date and they return. (This is why headers sort and don’t filter: a filter would fight the buckets, which already are the filters.)

Recommendation: A, with the bucket badge kept for search results only (where it already lives). All three changes are display-side — the pill and the dividers read data the rows already carry, and sorting is a client-side comparator on the list the tab already produced.
