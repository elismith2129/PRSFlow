What's different from the individual form
  • No photo ID, no Instagram, no "how did you hear".
  • Three people instead of one: billing, admin, A&R. Admin can be "same as billing" in one tap. A&R can add a second.
  • Two questions that feed billing straight away: PO required?, how to send invoices? — they land on the client's AP card.
  • T&Cs are signed for the company: printed name + title + "I'm authorised." The signature is kept the same way.
  • Opt-ins are for the signer, and email opt-in is new (the individual form only has SMS). Same consent log, same wording rules.
Build notes
  • One migration: registration_tokens.kind ('individual' | 'business'), clients.email_opt_in + _at, and the AP preference fields if the AP card doesn't already hold them.
  • Same route /register/<token>; the page asks the token which form to show. Links already sent keep working as individual.
  • Submit creates a label client + client_contacts rows (billing → admin type with role "Billing / AP"; admin → admin; A&R → anr). Email conflict check against the company's billing email, as today.
  • The modal replaces the direct "Send registration link" click in both places it exists: the lead panel and the client profile. Copy / email / text panel is unchanged after it.
  • Review banner and Confirm Client Account work as they do now; the print view (/register/view) gets a business block.
