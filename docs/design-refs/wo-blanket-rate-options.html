The whole-building blanket rate

Sometimes a client takes the entire building at one custom price per day. It's one line on the invoice and five rooms in the app. This is how that price becomes real money on the work order — money that reaches the daily numbers, the Financials chart, the SRS fee and the billing ladder without any of them being taught a new concept.

The live case: two days, all five Paramount rooms, $6,670/day = $13,340. Currently sitting as five separate work orders. It should be one.

1The allocation, on the real numbers

Pro-rata: sum the rack rates, divide the blanket by that sum, apply the factor to every room. Then the proof that this is the right basis — the factor comes out at exactly 0.92, and every share lands on a whole dollar with zero remainder. $6,670 was never an arbitrary number; it is rack minus 8%. Pro-rata doesn't approximate the deal, it reproduces it.

Why the invariant is not paranoia. Run the same allocation against the stale rate sheet (Studio A at $1,650, rack $7,150) and the rounded shares sum to $6,669.99 — a real penny short, on the first try, on live numbers. The remainder rule (largest room absorbs it) is what makes the sum exact every time instead of most of the time.

All three options produce identical data. They differ only in where you set the number and how visible it stays afterwards.

It seeds the ten rows and prices them in one action — which for a five-room two-day booking is the difference between ten hand-built rows and one press. 6Answered — and the one thing parked

All four closed by Eli in the same session. Three are rulings; the fourth is deliberately held open with a reminder owed.

OT — rack or discounted share? RACK. "OT is rack." The deal was struck on booked time and does not follow the client into the overrun. Studio B's 2 hrs over = 2 × $155, never 2 × $142.60. Settled — do not re-derive.
Can a bundle span venues? YES. "One WO can already have diff rooms." The WO is not venue-scoped and the projection segments by room, so Paramount + Encore on one blanket rate needs no new mechanism. Membership stays a chosen set of rows — never narrow it to "all rooms at this location."
Does the rate card become a table? YES — BUILD IT. A room_rates table auto-fills the rows, gives the allocation a real basis, and gives the client-facing rate sheet one source. The drift is already proven: Studio A quoted at $1,650 for months when it is $1,750.
The bundled calendar card PARKED — REMINDER OWED. "We'll come back to that, nothing for now, just remind me. There might be a cool way to do something there." Five cards currently each show a share rather than the blanket. Not dropped and not decided — raise this unprompted when the blanket rate is next picked up.
