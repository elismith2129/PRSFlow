how do I take one day off a hold

Open the hold's work order card and tap Remove hold. Tick only the days coming off and confirm — the rest of the hold stays. Removed days go to the Removed holds tab, where they can be recovered.

Why
  • It's what you asked for, and it's the shape people already know from every other app, so nobody needs telling.
