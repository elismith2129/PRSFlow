Two small fixes

Duties you can't read in Your List, and the Mark reviewed chip sitting on the status pills in the work order's day card. Both are cheap; each has a recommended pick.
2

Duties are cut off

The row is one line, ellipsised, with no tooltip and no way to open it. A long label like the LADWP one is unreadable. Scroll the lists: the boxes are fixed height either way (the list scrolls inside), so none of these grow the card.

Nothing to tap, nothing to learn, and it reads the same in the pop-up and on the dashboard tile. If a label is longer than two lines, it's a label that needs rewriting, not a bigger box.
