Calendar — keeping times on slim cards

Row height unchanged at 80px. Two failures to fix: a single-day card in a 3-lane row (24px) drops its times entirely, and a multi-day bar in any shared row (under 56px) loses its per-day cells and shows day one's times across the whole span. Every mock below is drawn at the real pixel heights the grid produces.

Today what's broken

Top row is three single-day sessions sharing one room. 24px each — name only, no times. Bottom row is a 4-day booking sharing with one other session: 37px, so no spine, and 6P–1A is day one's time stamped across all four days regardless of what the other days actually are.

Option A — Slim spine multi-day

The spine card survives the squeeze by dropping its staff line instead of the whole structure. Name bar on top, one line of per-day ranges underneath. Needs about 34px, so it covers the 2-lane case. Single-day cards get the range inline on the name line.

Option C — Name pinned left, times in their day columns multi-day

The name gets a fixed shaded block on the left edge of the bar; the remaining width is divided into day columns carrying their own ranges. One line, so it works all the way down to 24px, and every day keeps its time including the first.
