Actual vs billed — the session punch clock

Two new fields per studio day: when the client actually arrived and actually left. No money math ever reads them — billed stays booked, always, so nothing a runner does here can move an invoice. Every entry is WO-history-logged, and "% of booked time used" becomes a one-query report later. The options differ only in how a runner gets the time in. All of them live in the runner day sheet (and the same fields appear, editable, on the admin side).

A · Pure punch clock
Two buttons in the day sheet: Client arrived stamps the current time the moment it's tapped; it then arms Client left. Nothing to type, nothing to format — the most unfuckupable input there is. The weakness: a runner who forgets in the moment has no way to enter 12:00 PM at 3 PM, so forgetting = no data.

C · Punch first, correct anytimeRecommended
A's buttons and B's wells are the same control in two states. Empty = the big tap-to-stamp button (the in-the-moment path). Stamped = the dashed well showing the time with a tiny ✎ — tap to correct or to enter from memory (same smart-parse + preset list as every time field). Late entry works, in-the-moment works, and the dashed skin + the locked "billed as booked" line underneath make the two kinds of time impossible to confuse.

How the office reads it (all options)
On the admin WO, each day block grows one quiet line — actual times + hours used vs booked — and the same dashed wells are editable there (office can correct a runner's entry; WO history logs who). Never on the client PDF. Later, this is the utilization report: % of booked time used by client / room / month.

Recommendation: C. The punch is the habit you want to train (tap when they walk in — zero thought), and the ✎ correction is the honest fallback the 2 AM from-memory reality requires. Guard rails in every option: separate columns no money math can see, dashed visual skin so actual never reads as billed, the "billed as booked" line restating the law right where the runner is standing, and warn-never-block (an arrival before booked start or after booked end is allowed — it just shows). Stamp times round to 5 minutes so the data reads clean.
