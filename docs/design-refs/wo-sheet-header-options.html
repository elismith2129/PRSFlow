Day sheet header — room for the status

The per-day status pill joined a header row that was already full, so "3 of 9" folds into a column and the room code gets crushed at the edge. Two ways to give it room.

One row, no wrap, seven things. The two flexible items — "3 of 9" and the room code — are what give.

ATwo rows: where/when, then state Recommended

Row 1 is navigation and identity: ‹ date › · 3 of 9 · PRS B. Row 2 is the day's state: NOT SUBMITTED · Confirmed / Tentative / Cancelled. Anything we add later that's about the day's state (Reviewed, Times TBD) goes on row 2 without touching row 1.

Phone: row 1 already fits at 390px (the date chip is the only wide thing); row 2 wraps its two chips if it has to. Cost: split one <span> into two; the "for other days · 3/9" tail on the Agreed line becomes redundant with "3 of 9" sitting right above it — drop it.

BOne row, state pinned right

Keep one row: identity on the left (date, room, count), the status cluster pushed to the right with margin-left:auto, and "3 of 9" told not to wrap. Wraps to two rows by itself when there isn't room.
