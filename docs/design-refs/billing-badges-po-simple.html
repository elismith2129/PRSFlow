Billing badges + the simple PO flow

Two changes, one page. The COD bin badge comes to the billing side and replaces the three lights — the ladder is strictly linear, so one word carries the whole position. And a PO that arrives after approval is handled in a slim strip under the row — no modal, no re-approval, no trip to the work order.

The badge and the button can never disagree. Both read the same derivation the lights read today (the step, plus a standing rejection, plus the PO gate). A row that says NEEDS APPROVAL has, by definition, already been reviewed and invoiced — the lights said that in three cells; the badge says it in one. No new state, no migration.
1 · The list, re-badgedThe main change
In progress, default sort (by date, day dividers on — the CRM's idiom). The badge column sits where COD's bin badge sits. The colours: In progress is COD's exact blue, amber waits on someone in-house (review, invoice, approval), Awaiting PO is a lighter blue (waiting on the label), Approved and Paid are green (the Paid tab is rarely open, so they never sit together in practice), Not approved is hot. Buttons are never green — colour belongs to status; a button is a verb. The flag column stays for the exceptions that ride on top of a stage — drift, the owner's note.

The three lights are GONE from the billing side (your call — the badge already says where it is). Sent rows wear the blue SENT badge in Awaiting payment; Paid and Closed wear theirs, so search results — which span every bucket — need no extra chip. The COD side is untouched: its bins stay exactly as they are. Headers sort on click (arrow marks the column, click again to reverse); day dividers show only while sorted by date — sorted by Balance a date heading would lie about what follows it.

The whole life of a row, in buttons: nothing (review + approve happen elsewhere — the WO and the approval strip) → Add PO if one is needed → Download → Mark sent, which starts the aging clock and moves it to Awaiting payment → Mark paid there when the money lands. Four words, in order, never more than one at a time. A Not-approved row re-queues itself the moment the corrected invoice is dropped on it — no button.
2 · Adding a PO after approval — a strip, not a modal
The AWAITING PO row's button says the thing you actually came to do. Pressing Add PO unfolds a strip under the row — the row above stays visible the whole time, so you never leave the list. Type the PO, drop the re-issued invoice (the label usually re-dates it to the PO date — the date lives in the PDF, nothing else to type), Save & download. Then the button is Mark sent, exactly as if the PO had been there all along. The approval is untouched throughout — the money never moved, and the money is the only thing an owner signed.

Error after approval is a different door and stays automatic: touch the money and the row drops back to NEEDS APPROVAL on its own (drift), through review and a new invoice to the owner. The strip never appears on those rows — their button says Approve, not Add PO — so the two scenarios cannot be confused.
3 · Two silent bugs fixed underneath (no UI)
Found while scoping this; both ship with it.
1 · A replacement invoice could silently re-approve itself. Dropping a new PDF re-snapshotted the approved total, so an edit-then-reattach skipped the owner. Fixed: a replacement PDF never touches the snapshot — a pure swap keeps the approval (nothing moved), a swap after a money change still goes back to the owner.

2 · Re-downloading a sent package overwrote the record of what the client received. Fixed: once marked sent, a re-download serves the stored file, exactly as it went out.
One question: does a PO ever turn up AFTER the invoice was already sent — and if so, does a re-issued invoice actually go back to the label? If yes, the same strip opens from ⋯ on a sent row (“Add PO & re-send”), the aging clock keeping its original date. If that basically never happens, it isn’t built.
