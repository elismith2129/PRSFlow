Why here: the flag cell is spoken for (it's the row's "what happened last"), the action cell is "only ever the next real act" (Aug 11 ruling), and ⋯ is already the row's "more" corner. A note is neither a flag nor an act. Data: one small table, wo_collection_notes (work_order_id, author, text, created_at), append-only, RLS = owner/manager/billing/asst_manager, techs and runners never. Not on WorkOrderPopup, not in woPdf, not in the activity log — a separate table is what keeps it out of all three by construction.
