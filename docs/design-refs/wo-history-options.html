WO history — the paper original, back

Same data behind every option: one entry per save (who · when · what, from → to) and the creation snapshot as entry zero — the original work order, stored whole, never reconstructed. The options differ in where history lives and how an entry reads. Amber chip = the shared runner login; names = office profiles; the hot ⚠ is reserved for a change made after the invoice was attached, which billing already calls drift.

A · History modal — sentences, original pinned at the bottomRecommended
A small "⟲ History" link in the WO header opens this. Entries read as sentences, newest first — you scan it like a story of the booking. The creation snapshot renders as a real card, teal-edged, always last: scroll to the bottom and you're holding the paper original. Cheapest to read on a phone, and the WO itself stays untouched.

C · Original ⇄ Now — the compare view
One click from the History surface (either A or B): the creation snapshot on the left, the live WO on the right, changed fields tinted amber. This is the closest thing to lifting the pen marks off the paper — you see the original and today side by side without reading a feed. Worth having as the "Original" card's expand even if A wins; as the ONLY surface it hides the who-and-when, which is half your ask.

D · In-place markers — the change lives on the thing that changed
No separate surface: a small amber dot on any day card whose fields have moved since creation; tap it and that day's changes pop over, in place. Fastest answer to "did tonight change?" while standing in the building — but it can't tell the whole story (client and WO-level changes have no card to sit on), so it's a companion to A or B, not a rival.
