Status per day — confirmed Mon–Wed, tentative Thu–Fri, one work order

Makes sense, and it's the natural next step once the calendar treats the day as the unit. But be clear: this one is work-order logic — status drives the calendar colour, the runner hub, billing, the unsubmitted rule, and the guards we just built. The design below moves it to the day at exactly one layer so all of those keep working without each being rewritten.

1The work order: the top bar sets all days, each day can differ

The status bar at the top becomes All days — tap Confirmed and every day is confirmed. Each day card gets its own small pill. When days differ, the top bar reads Mixed and shows the count; tapping it again snaps everything back to one status.

TBD is a button, not a blank. On a tentative day the time well and each staff well show a dashed TBD; tap to make it explicit (warm), tap again or type to fill it. A confirmed day cannot be saved with Times TBD — the guard says so, same as today's "confirmed needs times." A blank staff slot already means "engineer, TBD" in the data; it just becomes visible. Cancelled day: the row stays (history), it's struck through, and it's zeroed at total time — never rewritten on the row. Kill fee is the WO discount, as now.

2The calendar, automatically

The projection already splits a run on room and on date gap; it splits on status too. Mon–Wed becomes one confirmed card, Thu one tentative card, Fri one cancelled card — same work order. Everything that reads booking colour (calendar, runner hub, TV walls, daily-ops, the unsubmitted rule) keeps working untouched.

4Order of work

1 — B1 calendar cards (display only, no SQL). 2 — TBD buttons (one column, display + one guard). 3 — Status per day (one column, projection + totals + guards). Three pushes, each usable on its own; the risky one last, on a branch with a preview you walk before it merges.
