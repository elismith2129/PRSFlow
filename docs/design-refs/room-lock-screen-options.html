The lock screen — three looks

What a client sees on the room's Mac when the room is locked. Each look uses different wording so you can pick the look and the words separately (the three wordings are listed again at the bottom).

The logo here is my redraw of the wave lockup, not your file. Send the real one for the build.

A — Centered

Recommended

Why: the calmest of the three. Logo, one line, one instruction. It reads from across the room and gives nobody anything to argue with.

Please see the front desk to extend your session and pay for overtime.
Your session is still open. Nothing has been lost.

Why you might want it: the logo is big and unmistakable, so it looks official and deliberate. Why not: the white panel is bright in a dark control room at 3am.

The three wordings

1Your paid time has ended.Please see the front desk to extend your session and pay for overtime.
2Your booked time is up.To keep working, see the front desk to add time. Overtime is paid before the room reopens.
3Paid time has ended.See the front desk to extend your session. Overtime is due before the room is unlocked.
"Your session is still open"On all three. It answers the first thing an angry client says ("you deleted my session"). It says open, not saved, because the lock does not save anything in Pro Tools.
The warning bannerNot in this mock. It can take the same wording once you pick, e.g. "Paid time ends in 14:59 · see the front desk to extend."
How close the build will beThe Mac draws this itself, not as a web page. The logo goes in as your image file; the type will be the Mac's own heavy font unless we ship Archivo with it.
