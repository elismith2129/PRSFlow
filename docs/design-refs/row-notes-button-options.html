The pick
  • A. The word "Notes" is what makes it findable; the count is the one fact worth carrying on the row. B's "who and when" is good information but it's a third thing to read on a row that already carries status, flag and two amounts — it can live in the hover card instead (it does, in A).
  • Hover card: newest three, initials + time, "and N more", "click to open and write" at the foot. 340px, drops down from the button, right-aligned so it never covers the amounts.
  • Click → the existing pop-up (composer at the top, the log under it, edit). Nothing new to build there.
  • The ✎ and the orange dot come out of the ⋯ cell. ⋯ keeps its menu.
What it costs / what to watch
  • No migration. The per-row count already loads on one channel (useCollectNoteCounts); the hover needs the last three notes, fetched on first hover per row and cached.
  • Phone / iPad: no hover. The button shows the count; tap opens the pop-up. Same as today, just findable.
  • The column is new in the row grid: between the client/session text and the flag. On narrow desktops the flag text already wraps; the button is fixed-width so it doesn't make that worse.
  • Sept 24 ruling holds: the row itself carries no note text. The preview is a hover card.
