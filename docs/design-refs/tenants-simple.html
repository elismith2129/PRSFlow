Tenants — how it works

Three rules. If you remember one thing: tenants pay rent, not work orders.

1

Rent is tracked on the Tenants tab. Three taps a month.

Every tenant, every month, one row. When the rent email goes out, tap Sent. When the money lands, tap Paid. When it's in QuickBooks, tap In QB. Unpaid past the 5th goes red.

Open→Sent→Paid · Sep 1→In QB ✓·  late:Open · 23 days

MBA's month runs the 16th → 15th, so his row is due the 16th and goes red on the 21st. Everyone else is the 1st.

2

Tenants don't have work orders. Except Mustard.

Camper, MBA and the rest: no WO, no day cards, nothing for runners, nothing in Billing. The room is just blocked on the calendar.

Mustard is a normal work order — runners fill his day card every day, the office approves it every day, exactly like a session. The only difference is the price: $0 room rate, 12 hours included, $165/hr overtime. His rent is on the Tenants tab; his OT and shared-runner hours bill through Billing like any other WO.

3

Financials does the per-day math itself.

Rent shows up on the revenue chart spread across the days of the month automatically. Nobody enters anything. (This is why Camper's and MBA's fake WOs go away — they only existed to feed the chart.)
