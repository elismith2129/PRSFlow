Multi-day sessions — "needs review" and "still running" at the same time

Today a four-day session flips to Needs review the moment day 1 is submitted, and stays there — even after day 1 is reviewed — until the last night. The badge says one thing when two are true. Every day already carries its own submit and review state, so this is a display and bucket-rule change, no new data.

AThe day strip on the badge Recommended

One badge, two facts: the stage word plus "day 2 of 4", and a strip under it — one segment per day. Green = reviewed, amber = submitted and waiting on you, red = a past day nobody submitted, grey = still ahead; today's segment has a ring. The bucket rule tightens to match: Needs review only while a submitted day is unreviewed. Review what's in and the row drops back to In progress until the next night lands.

BStay In progress, count the days owed

The row never leaves In progress until the last night. A warm chip on it says "1 day to review." Needs review becomes ended-sessions-only.

Why not: the dashboard's Needs review number stops meaning "what I owe today" — a lockout's day 2 is real review work and it would be hiding under In progress. Also nothing changes for you visually except the chip.

DRules, in one place

QuestionAnswer (option A)
When is a multi-day row in Needs review?Any day is submitted and not yet reviewed (locked). Same for a past day nobody submitted — that's review work too, and it wears the red flag.
When is it In progress?Every submitted day is reviewed and at least one day is still ahead (or tonight).
When does it move on to Needs invoice?Unchanged — the last day has passed and everything's reviewed (or you attach early, as today).
What does "review a day" mean?What it means today: open the WO, check the day, lock it. The Next button deep-links to that day's sheet instead of the top of the WO.
COD side?Same strip on the stage badge. Balance due still beats everything.
One-night sessions?Untouched. No strip, no counter.
