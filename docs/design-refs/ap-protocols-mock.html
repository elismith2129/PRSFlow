Client AP Protocols

How each label wants its invoice submitted. A major has one or more procedures its divisions share; an independent has its own.
What changed. The old screen was a flat 260px list of 26 procedure names, so "Sony — Arista / Alamo" had to carry the hierarchy in its own text and the divisions were invisible until you clicked. Here the shape is the layout: major → its procedures → the divisions using each. You can see at a glance that UMG is one procedure for everyone and Sony is four different ones.
