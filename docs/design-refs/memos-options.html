Memos

Write it once. It appears for the people it's for, the next time they open the app. They read it where it appears — no attachment to open — and sign with their initials. You see who has and who hasn't. The SOP gate already does this for one document; this is the same thing for a feed.

Rulings folded in. Soft then hard (one "later", then it blocks after 48 hours). Senders: owners and Fernando. Audience is strict — runners never see admin memos, runner memos never reach admin. In the app only, for now. A designed memo renders inline, graphics and all.

4Rules this locks in

One memo at a time, oldest unread first — a stack of five is read as five, not skimmed as one. The signature is the initials, pre-filled and shown large; pressing Sign records name, initials and time. "Later" works once per memo. After 48 hours from that press the memo blocks until signed, and the screen says why. Sending is final — a sent memo can be archived (hidden from the board, receipts kept) but not edited; a correction is a new memo. Nothing leaves the app — no email, no SMS — so the scoreboard shows last-open, because that is the honest limit of a pop-up. Designed pages are sandboxed — they render in a frame with no scripts, so a memo can't touch the app around it.
