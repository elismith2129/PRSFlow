Day sheet — final: OT as designation

One design, two OT states, both day types. The agreed window sits right under the date so the baseline is always on screen. The runner touches only the clock — hours and dollars are computed, and the billing block always separates agreed charges from the overage, which is the whole designation. The internal OT pre-approved flag (set by the office, never printed) decides what running over does: approved → it just bills, stated plainly (phone 1, phone 3). Not approved on a COD session → the hot desk prompt: extend & collect now (phone 2). Not approved on a billing session → same card, but the button reads "Flag for office" instead of collecting.
