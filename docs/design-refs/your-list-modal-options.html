Your list — the full modal, and adding your own

Click the Your-list box on the dashboard → a large modal with everything on you, untruncated, grouped by whether it's due. Tick things there. A + Add composer makes a one-time task or a recurring one (daily / weekly with days / monthly with a day). Your own items can be edited or removed; the ones Eli set wear a lock. 2+ Add — one composer for a task or a recurring item

Opens at the top of the list. Type it, choose One-time or Repeats. Repeats reveals the cadence; weekly shows day pills (pick several), monthly shows a day-of-month. The line at the bottom reads the schedule back in words before you commit. The same form opens on ✎ for one of yours.

The three shapes of the composer. Weekly = day pills, several allowed. Monthly = a day number (1–28 typed; "last day" as an option so the 31st doesn't skip short months). Under the hood (so the mock is honest): the recurring engine already exists — myday_duties has cadence daily/weekly/monthly + due_days, and the dashboard's lateness/backlog logic reads it. Two columns make it personal: owner_profile_id (NULL = the role's duty, as today) and created_by. Locked = created by an owner, or seeded (created_by NULL). One-time tasks are what dashboard_tasks already is — assigned to self. The modal is one component fed by the same queueRows() the dashboard box uses, so they can't disagree.
