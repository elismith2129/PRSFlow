The mark gets its colour back

The carved redesign made the wave mark pure monochrome (currentColor at .35/.6/1) and the home-screen icon was never regenerated, so the phone icon still wears the old teal/lime. Eli's ruling: colour returns to the brand mark as a sanctioned exception to Law 3 — the same shape of exception the Flo glow is to the no-glow rule. One mark, brand and AI. Five options below. Each shows the app icon (large + favicon scale), the login/splash lockup, and the rail lockup. Toggle both registers before picking — the in-app mark must live on paper and in the dim room.

What the pick decides

Spec amendment. The winning treatment becomes a sanctioned Law 3 exception: "the brand mark carries its own colour — the one place non-status colour exists," recorded in PRSFLO-DESIGN-SPEC.md the way the Flo glow is recorded against §7. Everything else stays ink, paper, wash.

One mark everywhere. `components/PRSFloIcon.tsx` is updated once; login, splash, rail, SOP gate, inquiry and the Flo box header all inherit. `scripts/generate-icons.js` is rewritten to the same art and re-run — favicons, apple-touch, 192/512, plus the runner set (runner keeps its differentiation: the top wave goes orange in every option).

Lime note (option C/E). On the charcoal icon and dark register, lime `#c8f04e` sings. On warm paper it's near-invisible at small sizes, so light mode uses a deepened lime (`#9dbd2e`-ish, exact value tuned in build) — same identity, legible ink-side. Options A and B don't need this; their colours hold on both grounds.

No glow, no shadows, flat fills. All five options are flat by construction. The old icon's radial glow and drop shadows do not return in any of them — colour is the richness now, not atmosphere. (Flo's aurora stays the only glow.)
