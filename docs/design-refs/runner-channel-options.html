Notes — a room you go to, not a box on the hub

Runner report: @ people like Slack, get pinged when tagged and given a start time, a General channel. Eli: "spaces you want to live in — easy nav, easy understand, easy input." The current channel has the right data and the wrong container: it's a scrolling window wedged under six tiles. This gives it a door and a room.

The one idea that turns chat into work: a mention with a time is a task. "@Hunter 3pm set up B for Lainey" stays in the channel as a message and lands on Hunter's hub as a task with 3:00 PM on it. He checks it off there; the channel shows it done. One gesture, two surfaces.

Rejected on Eli's rule. The features fit; the space doesn't. A room you live in isn't a scrolling box under six tiles — you can't see more than three messages, the composer fights the Submit buttons for the bottom of the screen, and General would be a fourth thing crammed into the same window. A page costs one tap and gives the whole screen back.
