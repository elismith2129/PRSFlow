Cancelled sessions, kill fees, and the Closed reason

Three changes that belong together. A cancelled session stops disappearing from billing; the work order gains a discount line so a 50% kill fee is two keystrokes; and closing an invoice asks why, with a note.

The discount cannot live in payments. payment_rows are money received — paid = Σ amount, balance = grand − paid. A discount entered there would make the work order read as PAID when nothing arrived, and a $3,000 kill fee would show a zero balance in AR while the client still owed every penny. It goes on the charge side, where it reduces what is owed.

1Why they vanish today

NON_SESSION_STATUSES = ['tour','tech','open_hours','cancelled'] — one constant doing two unrelated jobs. Tour, Tech and Open Hours are calendar events where nothing is ever charged, so excluding them is right. Cancellations got filed alongside them, and they are often charged. Billing filters them out before the pipeline, and a session cancelled before it ever had a work order never gets one at all.

Dropping 'cancelled' from that list is the actual fix. Everything below is what makes the fix useful.

2The discount control — two shapes

Both sit in the work order's totals panel, which is where a person checks a number against an invoice. Both store the same three columns. The question is only how much ceremony it takes.

A · Always-visible line recommended
A permanent row in the totals block reading No discount until you touch it. Click the amount, get a %/$ toggle and a field. On a CANCELLED session it pre-fills 50% and labels itself Cancellation — 50% kill fee, because that is the case it exists for — but it is one field on every work order, so a goodwill credit or a comped hour has a home too.

Nothing says this session was cancelled and is being billed in full.
The card fee doesn't enter into this. An earlier draft of this page claimed the 3% had to be taken on the discounted subtotal rather than the full one. Wrong — the fee is attached to each payment, not to the invoice, because clients pay in increments and OT lands after the fact. cardFeeOfCharged derives it from what the terminal actually charged, and computeWoTotals sums payment_rows.fee_amount. A $2,000 kill fee paid as two $1,000 card payments carries 3% on each as it is taken. So the discount reduces the base obligation, card fees accrue per payment, and the two never interact: grand = studio + engineer + rentals − discount + Σ fees.

3Closing an invoice — the reason, and the message

invoice_closed_reason already exists but holds only written_off or voided, and nothing can say why. invoice_reject_note is the precedent for the free-text half — same shape, so this is an extension rather than a new idea.

The close dialog
Reason is required, note is optional — except on Other, where a reason that explains nothing is worse than no reason at all, so the note becomes required.
