Billing hub — "what needs me today" vs "what hasn't been checked"

The two-word badge (v1.33.3) tells you a running multi-day has something unreviewed. It can't tell you whether that something is last night (expected — today's work) or a night from three days ago that slipped (a miss), and once you've reviewed yesterday nothing on the row says so. Four ideas below, then the recommended combination as it would look on the In progress tab. Same sample rows throughout; "today" is Wed Sep 23.

AThe badge names the night Recommended

"Needs review" splits into what kind of review it is. LAST NIGHT is amber — it landed while you slept, it's today's work, expected. UNCHECKED · N is hot — nights older than last night that nobody has reviewed, or that were never submitted; that's a miss, and it should look like one. A running multi-day with everything reviewed says REVIEWED · Tue in grey: still in progress, nothing for you. One-night sessions keep the plain word.

BThe flag cell carries the last act Recommended, with A

Today the flag cell only ever says what the runner did. It should say the most recent act on the row, whoever did it: amber when a runner turned a night in and it's waiting on you; green when the office reviewed it and nothing is waiting. That's the missing "yesterday is reviewed" stamp — and the trail: who checked, when.

DThe tab answers the two questions in its headings Recommended, with A + B

Row-level fixes still make you read every row. The In progress tab already opens in queue order under stage headings ("Needs review · 6"). The counts in the headings are the two numbers you asked for; the tab count on the dashboard money tile could mirror them (hot count · amber count).

+While I was in there — two smaller things on the same tab

The "all reviewed" badge is the odd one out

In progressall reviewed"In progress" is a state; "all reviewed" is a report. Every other badge says what the row needs (Sep 22 ruling: Approved → Ready to send). Option A's REVIEWED · Tue fixes this the same way — it says the last thing done, so the reader knows there's nothing to do.

The flag cell is the runner's line — make it the last act line

✓ Reviewed thru Sep 22Today it can say "Never submitted" (red) or "Submitted by" (amber) or, once the office covers a missed night, the green "trail closes" line from v1.39.3. Option B is just extending that same trail rule to ordinary review: the cell is always the most recent thing that happened, coloured by who it's waiting on. One rule, four states, no new column.

Recommendation: A + B + D. A makes the badge honest for a multi-day (it names the night, and a miss looks like a miss). B stamps the review so "did I do yesterday" is on the row. D puts the two questions in the first two headings so you don't read rows at all until you've decided where to start. C stays rejected. Build order: A + D first (derivation + QUEUE_RANK + labels, no migration, one session); B second (migration for locked_by/locked_at, then the flag cell).
