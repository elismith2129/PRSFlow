How OT works
  • OT hours = booked hours − covered hours (17 − 12 = 5). Booked, not "actually here" — the house rule.
  • Type a number in OT hours to override (a negotiated 3h, say); clear it to go back to auto.
  • No OT rate set → no OT, same as today.
  • Runners see the card read-only, OT line included — no prices to edit.
What stays invisible
  • The $15,000 and the OT are still split across the five rooms by their normal rates, behind the scenes — Financials, room numbers and SRS keep working.
  • Nothing on the work order, the PDF or the runner app shows the split. The invoice shows one line for the day + one OT line.
  • Existing blanket days with a typed OT dollar amount keep it until someone sets an OT rate.
