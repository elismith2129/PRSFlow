# HR & payroll documents

Operating documents for PRG's HR and payroll procedures. These are business documents, not code
— they live here so every Claude session can reference them and so revisions are versioned.

**The technical spec is `docs/HR-SPEC.md`.** This folder holds the human-facing documents it
implements.

## Standing rules that must not be softened

These appear across several documents and are the ones most likely to get quietly eroded:

1. **No punch is edited without written confirmation from the employee.** A submitted PRSFlo
   request counts. A saved text or email counts. Verbal does not.
2. **All hours worked are always paid.** A missed punch never delays or reduces pay.
3. **Pay is never used as a consequence.** Never dock, withhold, delay, or cut hours over a
   missed punch — that converts a coaching issue into a wage claim.
4. **Never adjust a punch to make a meal break look compliant.** Pay the one-hour premium
   instead. The premium is an ordinary cost; falsifying a time record is not.
5. **A manager never approves an employee's timecard on their behalf.**
6. **Involuntary termination means the final check exists before the conversation** (LC 201).
   Requires Eli's approval and 48 hours' notice to Lynair.

## Interim operation

**Every procedure works on paper today.** Each P document carries an "Until PRSFlo ships" box

identical columns so it imports later.

## Scope

These are operating procedures, not legal advice. Each R document carries a scope note naming
what needs professional review. Before go-live, an employment attorney or CalChamber HR advisor
should review: final pay (R02 §2.1), whether the studios fall under the WPV mandate (R04 §3),
the notices-at-hire list (R02 §3), and the missed punch ladder against the handbook (R03 §4).
