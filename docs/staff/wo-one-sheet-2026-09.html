Rates live in the app. Rooms arrive at the right number.

The table fills a rate when you add a room and no rate is there yet — Add dates, the Seed panel, or picking a room on a blank row. It never changes a rate already on a row, and it never touches the lead form. Owners, managers and billing can edit it under the rail's Admin → Rates.

Hourly is always the day rate ÷ 10. You only ever type the day rate.
Day cards

A card is a day, not a room.

ERS A · ERS B Wed, Sep 9
9:00 AM – 7:00 PM 10h · Blanket rate $4,500/day
2NDWyatt Sayre · 8:30 AM – 7:00 PM

Add a second room to a day that already exists and it joins that day's card — "ERS A · ERS B". A new date gets its own card. Tap the card to open the day sheet, where times, staff, rate, hours included and the blanket switch all live.

×A duplicate staff line? Press its × on the card. A room added to a staffed day shouldn't add one — but old sessions may have them.

Day rates, hours included, overtime

A day rate buys 12 hours unless you say otherwise.

TypeDay Hr
Room rate$3,150
Hours included9
OT rate$315
9:00 AM – 7:00 PM · 10h · OT 1h · $315.00

Leave Hours included blank for a normal lockout. Type 9 for a 9–6 day, 4 for a short event. Overtime starts after that number, at the OT rate (normally the day rate ÷ 10).

Hourly rows have no overtime. An 8-hour session that runs 9 bills 9 hours. Overtime is a day-rate thing.
Cancelled sessions & kill fees

Cancelled still bills. The Discount line is where the kill fee goes.

Discount %$ 50 Cancellation — kill fee −$1,575.00

A cancelled session comes through to Billing like any other. Charging a kill fee: type the percent or amount and a short reason — it prints on the invoice. Not charging at all: close it in Billing and pick a reason.

It never fills itself in. Sometimes a cancellation is billed in full.
