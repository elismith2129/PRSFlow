The big one

Notes is a room now. Tag people. A tag plus a time makes a task.

Notes used to be a box on the hub. It's its own page — tap the Notes card on the hub to go in. Two tabs: your studio and General, which every studio and the office can see. Newest messages are at the bottom, like Messages.

  • Search box at the top. Type a word, every group opens to the hits.
  • "40/113 counted" in the title — so you don't close a tab and forget one you skipped. Orange until it's all counted.
  • A dashed LOW · since 9/12 means it was low last night, not tonight. Tap once to confirm it's still low, twice if it's been restocked.
  • Waters, bagels, cups and lids open the full keyboard so you can type "3 / 3" and "5+". Everything else keeps the number pad.
  • No more "+ Add item". If something's on the shelf but not on the list, tap Missing an item? at the bottom — it goes to Eli and the office adds it. (Two people adding the same water made a mess at ARS.)

Petty cash

Closers: count the box and type it.

Opening balance$200.00
Cash out-$34.50
Closing balance$165.50
Counted at close160.00
Short by $5.50 vs the ledger

The opener's count used to make the tile say "Submitted" all day, so nobody could tell if the closer had counted. Now there's a Counted at close box. Count the cash, type what you find, and the line under it says whether it matches the ledger.

A mismatch is not something to fix on the spot. Type what's really there — the office sees the difference in the morning.

On the hub the Petty cash tile reads "Opened · count at close" until the closer types a count.
