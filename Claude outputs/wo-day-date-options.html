Moving a day — the date is a control, not a caption

Today the day sheet shows ‹ Thu, Oct 15 › with a tiny ✎ date. The arrows step between the WO's days, so on a one-day WO they're disabled and look dead; the pencil is an invisible browser date field that iPad Safari often won't open. The card view has nothing. Under the hood the move already works — every row of the day (room + staff) takes the new date. Only the control is bad.

The correction is almost always off by one day. So the two buttons the thumb finds first should be − day and + day, with a real picker behind the date for anything further.

Big targets. − / + move this day's rows one day. Tapping the date opens our own month grid (not the browser's). The card gets the same control small, so a wrong day is fixed without opening EDIT. Approved days show the date plain — locked, like every other edit.

BA "Move this day" sheet

The header stays as it is but the date becomes one big tappable pill. Tapping it slides up a sheet: −1 day · +1 day · the month grid. Same on the card.

Cleaner header, one more tap. Every move — including the 90% case of "it's tomorrow, not today" — costs a tap to open the sheet before the tap that does it. A puts −/+ on the surface for exactly that case, and keeps the grid one tap away for the rest. B is the right shape if the header ever gets crowded; it isn't yet.
