Two decisions, three small options each. All of them use the name and time already on the row since v1.33.2. Nothing here needs SQL.

1Where "submitted by" lives

1A · The flag cell says it. Recommended Same column that carries "Runner never submitted" — so that column reads as the runner's line: who did, or who didn't. Badge unchanged.

Catch: "Submitted" is the runner's word for their act; "Needs review" is Fernando's word for his. Renaming the stage to the runner's act means the badge stops telling Fernando what he owes.

Reading it: "2/20" = two nights turned in out of twenty booked. The flag cell names the latest one. One-night sessions carry no counter. The red "never submitted" flag still wins the cell when a past day is missing.

2C · Badge stays plain, the flag cell carries the day. Badge just says NEEDS REVIEW or IN PROGRESS; the flag says "Day 2 submitted · Hunter · ends Oct 5".

Quietest option. Works, but "still running" is only readable if you notice the end date.

3Either way, the rule underneath

Needs review = at least one submitted day you haven't reviewed (or a past day nobody submitted). In progress = everything that's in is reviewed and there are nights still to come. Today a lockout sits in Needs review for its whole run; with this it only shows up when there's actually a day for Fernando to look at.
