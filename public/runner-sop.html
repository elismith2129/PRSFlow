Chapter 06

Submit. Every night.

Session over? Submit before you leave. It is the most important button in the app.

The green button

Why

What saves by itself, and what does not

Needs Save or Submit: times, arrived / left, No show, staff, song title, rentals, payments, the printed name and signature, session notes, the Needs Attention words.

Saves by itself: equipment pills, the “what was wrong” note (when you tap out of the box) and its photo, food receipts, Needs Attention photos.

Not sure? Tap Save. It saves everything and takes you back to your studio page.

Chapter 14

The rules

If you remember nothing else, remember these.

Nine rules

  • Your PIN, your name. Never work under someone else’s sign-in.
  • Pick the studio you are standing in.
  • On a work order, nothing is saved until Save or Submit. ← and Cancel lose it.
  • Ran over? Change End. Left early? Leave it.
  • Always fill in Arrived and Left. Or No show.
  • COD: signature, the right number, Save.
  • Submit every session before you leave. Late beats never.
  • Something wrong? Write it in Needs Attention, or Raise a flag. Everything else goes in Notes.
  • Count the box. Type the real number. Even when it is short.

If something goes wrong

I submitted with a mistakeOpen the work order. Fix it. Tap Update submission. No penalty.
I forgot to submit last nightIt is on top of your studio page until the next morning. Open it, fill in Arrived and Left, submit. Already gone? Tell the office in Notes.
The day I need is lockedThe office approved it. Tell the office in Notes.
The cash box is shortCount again. Type the real number. Add a note.
