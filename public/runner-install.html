Get the Runner app on your phone

Six taps, one minute. After this it lives on your home screen and opens like any other app — use Safari for these steps.

1

Open the link

Tap the link we texted you — or type it into Safari:

prsflow.paramountrecording.com/runner
2

Put in your passcode

Your 6-digit PIN — same one every shift. Don’t have one yet? Ask the office.

3

Tap the ••• button

Once you’re in (you’ll see the four studios), tap the three dots at the bottom-right of Safari.

4

Tap Share

In the menu that opens, tap Share (the square with the arrow).

5

Tap “Add to Home Screen”

Scroll down the share sheet to Add to Home Screen — bottom of the list.

6

Tap Add

Leave Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add in the top corner. You can rename it “Runner” first if you like.

From now on: open it from your home screen, not Safari. It remembers your login and your studio. If the icon ever shows a plain letter tile instead of the wave, delete it and do these steps again.

That’s it. Questions → ask the office, or drop it in your studio’s Runner notes.
