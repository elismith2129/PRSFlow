Guardrails you will notice

  • Change the money after approval and it un-approves. The owner approved a figure, not whatever it becomes.
  • A sent invoice never changes quietly. The only way to restart it is Pull it back.
  • These leave no line in History: Mark sent, Mark paid, Pull it back, Close and Reopen. If it matters later, put a line in ✎.

Chapter 06

Undo, close, delete

Everything rare lives behind the ⋯ on a row. What you see there depends on where the row is.

The ⋯ on a row

Deleting, and getting it back

Good to know

  • Only you, the manager and the owners can delete. The work order itself has no Delete button. A hold has Remove hold instead: next chapter.
  • Gaps in the WO numbers do not mean something was deleted. Numbers are sometimes skipped.
Chapter 07

Removing a hold

A hold is a Tentative session. It comes off the calendar from its own work order, one day or all of it.

Remove hold

The Removed holds tab

It will say no when

  • The hold has an invoice or a payment on it, or was completed. Delete it from the hub instead.
  • Someone changed the hold while your window was open. Close it and open Remove hold again.

Chapter 13

The rhythm

The whole job, by when it comes around.

Every morning
  • Dashboard: Flo's red lines, then Your list.
  • Daily Ops: every WO not submitted badge chased and ticked.
  • Hub, In progress: clear Needs review.
  • Needs invoice: QuickBooks invoice made and dropped on the row.
  • COD: Balance due is empty, or you know why not.
Through the day
  • Ready to send: Download, email, Mark sent the same day.
  • Not approved: fix, re-drop, Replace it.
  • Mark paid the day money lands.
  • A line in ✎ every time you chase someone.
Every week
  • Awaiting payment, oldest first. Chase everything over 31 days.
  • Awaiting PO: chase each label.
The 1st
  • SRS: pay what is in Owed and mark each one paid.
Month-end
  • Petty cash: each studio, read "Nights the box didn't match", then Print or Download CSV.
Rent
  • Tenants: send by the date on the row, Mark sent, Mark paid, In QB.
Chapter 14

Help & words

When something looks wrong

A number that can't be rightOpen the work order and read the rows. Every total is worked out from them.
"Paid" is more than the totalA card payment where the terminal amount was typed into Toward balance. The red line under the payment offers the fix.
A work order has vanishedHub → page ⋯ → Deleted work orders. For a hold: the Removed holds tab. Then tell Eli.
Mark sent or Mark paid by mistakeRow ⋯ → Pull it back. It also removes the invoice and the approval. If it says only an owner can, ask Eli or Adam-Mike.
A PDF on the wrong rowThat row's ⋯ → Remove the attached invoice. Then drop it where it belongs.
A red "NOT saved" messageThe save failed and the app is saying so. Try again. If it keeps happening, tell Eli.
The manual doesn't match the screenThe app changed and the manual didn't. Say so.
