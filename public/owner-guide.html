PRSFlo · The Owner's Page

Your whole day,
in three stops.

Three screens run the business side of this app: the dashboard, the CRM, and the billing hub. This page is the map — what every color means, how the pipeline moves, and where the two calls that belong to an owner live: approvals and COD.

Stop 1 · Every morning

The Dashboard

Open the app — the dashboard is the front page, and it's the whole operation at a glance: tonight's rooms, the pipeline, the money.

Flo's briefing opens by itself each morning. Flo reads the night's notes, the open flags, the holds and the money pipeline, and reports in a few short lines. Lines with a small word beside them — CRM →, NOTES → — jump straight to the thing itself.

The THE MONEY box is the billing pipeline live. Four tiles:

COD OUT
Red — cash owed at the desk. Sessions that pay day-of and haven't yet. The number an owner watches closest.
NEEDS REVIEW
Amber — the billing desk's stage. Work orders in from the runners, being checked line by line before they become invoices.
NEEDS APPROVAL
Blue — waiting on an owner's sign-off. Reviewed, numbers checked, ready for your call. Blue means you, everywhere in this app.
READY TO SEND
Green — approved and moving. On the way out the door.
The read
Red is money waiting. Blue is waiting on you.
Stop 2 · The pipeline

The CRM

CRM in the left-hand menu. Every person who wants to book the studio lives here, colored by temperature:

UNCONTACTED
Brand new — nobody has spoken to them yet. Speed matters most here.
HOT
Ready to book — asking about dates, prices, rooms. Followed up every few days on a timer that doesn't forget.
WARM
Interested, still deciding. Same timers, longer leash.
COLD
Went quiet. Cold isn't dead — these get re-invited by email on a weekly cycle.
BOOKED
Closed. A client on the calendar.

Needs Action is the working list — the leads due for a touch today, worst-waiting first. Every conversation gets logged with a CONTACT press, stamped with who and how. That log is what feeds Flo's morning read on the pipeline: who's been called, what's overdue, what went cold overnight.

The owner's read
The Needs Action number should breathe — up with new leads, down as they're worked. If it climbs and stays, the briefing will show you exactly where.
Stop 3 · Approvals & the money

The Billing Hub

Billing in the left-hand menu. When invoices are waiting on a sign-off, a counter sits on the menu item itself — the hub is asking for you.

The page title is two pipelines: BILLING and COD — press either word to switch. Billing is invoiced clients: labels, companies, net terms. COD is pay-at-the-desk, day of session.

Every invoice wears its stage. The life of one, in order:

IN PROGRESS→ NEEDS REVIEW→ NEEDS APPROVAL→ APPROVED→ PAID
GREY
In motion — the session is running or the paperwork hasn't started. Dormant by design, not a signal.
AMBER
Needs review — the billing desk verifies the runner's numbers against the night.
BLUE
Needs approval — the owner's stage. Open it, review the numbers, press Approve. If something's off, Not approved sends it back to billing with your note attached.
GREEN
Approved, sent, or paid — moving on its own.
RED
A stopped pipeline. Balance due on a COD means the session happened and the money didn't — same-day conversation. Not approved means an owner sent it back and billing owes a fix.
The owner's calls
Blue — review and Approve, or bounce it back with a note.
Red on COD — money uncollected. It gets chased today.
The rhythm: Flo in the morning · clear the blue · watch the red.
WO — "work order": the invoice worksheet a session lives on.
COD — pay at the desk, the day of the session.
Hold — a date penciled in but not confirmed yet.
Flo — the app's assistant. The wave icon. She reads the record; she never invents a number.
