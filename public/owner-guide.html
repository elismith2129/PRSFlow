PRSFlo · The Owner's Page

Your whole day,
in three stops.

The app is big, but your part of it is small and always the same: read Flo in the morning, keep an eye on the pipeline, and approve what's waiting for you. This page walks the three screens you'll live on. Everything else in the app can wait for someone else.

Stop 1 · Every morning

The Dashboard

Open the app. You're already here — the dashboard is the front page.

Flo's briefing pops up by itself each morning. Flo is the app's assistant — she reads the night's notes, the open problems, and the money pipeline, and gives you a few short lines. Read them. Some lines have a little word next to them like CRM → — press it and the app takes you straight to that thing.

Then look at THE MONEY box. Four tiles, four colors:

COD OUT
Red — cash owed at the desk. Sessions that were supposed to pay at the studio and haven't. This is your number to watch.
NEEDS REVIEW
Amber — billing's homework. Work orders waiting for the billing desk to check the numbers. Not your job, but you can see the pile.
NEEDS APPROVAL
Blue — waiting on YOU. Invoices that are checked and just need an owner's OK. Blue means you, everywhere in this app.
READY TO SEND
Green — done and moving. Approved, on their way out the door.
Remember one thing
Red is money waiting. Blue is you.
Stop 2 · The pipeline

The CRM

Press CRM in the left-hand menu. This is every person who wants to book the studio — the pipeline. Each lead has a temperature:

UNCONTACTED
Brand new — nobody has talked to them yet. These get called fast.
HOT
Ready to book — asking about dates, prices, rooms. Don't let these sit.
WARM
Interested but thinking. We check in every few days.
COLD
Went quiet. The app re-invites these automatically — nothing to do.
BOOKED
The win. They're a client now, on the calendar.

Needs Action is the tab that matters — it's the short list of leads that need a call today. The staff works that list; the timers behind it never forget a lead. When anyone talks to a lead, they press CONTACT on it and log what happened — that's the whole discipline, and it's how Flo knows who's been called and who's rotting.

Your part here
Watch the big number. If Needs Action grows and stays grown, the floor isn't making its calls — and Flo will name names in your morning briefing.
Stop 3 · Your approvals & the money

The Billing Hub

Press Billing in the left-hand menu. When invoices are waiting for your OK, a little counter sits right on that menu item — that's your signal to come here.

The page title shows two words: BILLING and COD. They are two pipelines — press either word to switch. Billing = clients we invoice (labels, companies). COD = pay at the desk, day of session.

Every invoice on this page wears a colored badge. The life of an invoice, in order:

IN PROGRESS→ NEEDS REVIEW→ NEEDS APPROVAL→ APPROVED→ PAID
GREY
Still being worked — the session is running or hasn't been touched. Nothing for anyone yet.
AMBER
Needs review — the billing desk checks the runner's numbers. Their job, not yours.
BLUE
Needs approval — this one is YOURS. Open it, glance at the numbers, press Approve. If something looks wrong, press Not approved and it goes back to billing with your note.
GREEN
Approved, sent, or paid. Moving on its own.
RED
A problem. Balance due on a COD means the session happened and the money didn't — chase it the same day. Not approved means an owner bounced it back.
Your two jobs on this page
1 · Open the blue ones and press Approve.
2 · When the COD word turns red, someone owes us cash — ask about it today.
That's the whole job:
read Flo in the morning · clear the blue · watch the red.
