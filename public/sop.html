How the timers work
Every Hot and Warm lead has a countdown timer running in the background. The timer controls when a lead gets demoted. Logging a touch does not reset it. Only clicking Keep Hot? or Keep Warm? resets it.
🔥 HOT TIMER — 5 days
Lead created as Hot → 5-day timer starts
Day 5 arrives → Keep Hot? button appears on the card
Click Keep Hot? → timer resets, lead stays Hot for another 5 days
Don't click it → lead auto-demotes to Warm immediately
☀ WARM TIMER — 3 days
Lead demoted to Warm → 3-day timer starts
Day 3 arrives → Keep Warm? button appears on the card
Click Keep Warm? → timer resets for another 3 days
Don't click it → lead auto-demotes to Cold/Dead
✓
8am daily reset — every morning, all Hot and Warm leads reappear in Needs Action. That's your daily call list.
✓
Contact ≠ timer reset — logging a contact removes the lead from Needs Action for the day but the demotion timer keeps counting. Only Keep Hot? / Keep Warm? resets the clock.
✗
Don't ignore Keep Hot? / Keep Warm? — if you see that button and don't click it, the lead demotes automatically. If they're still worth pursuing, click it.
Working the Needs Action view
Needs Action is your daily call list. Every morning at 8am, every Hot and Warm lead reappears here. Work through it top to bottom — Uncontacted first, always: contact brand-new inquiries the same day, and escalate to upper management if you can't handle one. Then work your Hot leads. Each lead has a Contact button. Here's exactly what happens when you click it.
1
Click Contact on a lead
An inline prompt expands below the lead card. You don't leave the page — it all happens inline.
2
Select contact method
Choose how you reached them: Call · Text · Email. This gets logged in the notes as [5/21 2:30pm] ES - Call
3
Update temperature (optional)
If the lead's status changed based on the conversation, update it here: Hot · Warm · Cold/Dead. Changing status resets the demotion timer. Leave it if nothing changed.
4
Add notes (optional)
Any context worth capturing — what they said, next steps, anything relevant. Keep it brief and specific. This is what the next person reads.
5
Click Log Contact
Lead disappears from Needs Action. It'll reappear tomorrow at 8am if it's still Hot or Warm. The demotion timer keeps running in the background.
UNCONTACTED leads
Work these first, every time. A fresh inquiry sitting uncontacted is the most expensive thing in the CRM. Same-day contact, no exceptions. Escalate to management immediately if you can't handle it.
HOT leads
Work these right after uncontacted. A HOT lead sitting untouched in Needs Action is money walking out the door. Same-day contact, no exceptions.
WARM leads
Work these after HOT is cleared. Consistent presence is what moves a Warm lead toward booking. Keep it personal — don't just check a box.
Keep Hot? / Keep Warm?
If this button appears on a card, the demotion timer has expired. Click it to reset the timer and keep them at their current status. Ignore it and they demote automatically.
How to work the CRM
Training Guide · Clients
A booked lead becomes
a client for life.
The Clients page is the permanent record for everyone who books with Paramount. ID, agreements, contact info, history — it all lives here. A session should never happen without a full profile on file.
How a client profile gets created
01
Book in CRM
Lead confirms. Click Book Client → New Client. A unique registration link is generated.
02
Client Registers
Send the link. The client fills out their own profile, uploads ID, and signs T&Cs. You don't enter this for them.
03
Review Profile
Check immediately. ID uploaded? T&Cs signed? Phone and email present? Missing anything — flag it now.
Managing client profiles

✓ Always
